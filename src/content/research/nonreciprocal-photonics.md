questions:
  - Can on-chip isolation be achieved with compact, integrable magneto-optical or dichroic materials?
  - How do parity-time symmetry and gain/loss interact with magneto-optical nonreciprocity in coupled resonators?
  - Can valley-selective responses in 2D materials provide magnet-free, all-optical nonreciprocity?
---

Nonreciprocal devices such as isolators and circulators enforce one-way flow of light and are essential in photonic systems, yet they are difficult to miniaturize and integrate. My doctoral research explored several routes to on-chip nonreciprocity: ring resonators integrating dichroic magneto-optical materials, non-Hermitian (parity-time symmetric) double-ring systems, and all-optical nonreciprocity from valley polarization in monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides.
