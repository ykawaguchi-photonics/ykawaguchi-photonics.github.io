Flat optics replaces curved refractive surfaces with thin, lithographically defined metasurfaces. Beyond miniaturization, a metalens offers design freedom that conventional lenses do not — including strong, engineerable chromatic behavior.

Published work in this area includes a stereo metalens rangefinder that exploits the wavelength dependence of focal length to extend its measurement range, and the development of a full-color metalens targeting near-diffraction-limited focusing.
