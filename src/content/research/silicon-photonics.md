Photonic integrated circuits bring many optical functions onto a single chip. My work spans integrated ring-resonator devices, silicon-compatible Ge photodiodes and Si/Ge heterostructures, and plasmonic structures in highly doped GeSn on silicon.

On the computational side, I maintain an open-source [photonics simulation toolkit](/projects/photonics-simulation-toolkit/) that builds and validates PIC components with Meep FDTD and GDSFactory layouts, then composes them into circuit-level models.
