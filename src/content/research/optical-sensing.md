---
title: Optical Sensing
short: Optical sensing
order: 6
description: Integrated refractive-index sensors combining plasmonic nanostructures with Ge photodiodes, and optical ranging with flat optics.
questions:
  - How can a plasmonic sensing element and a photodetector be integrated collinearly into one compact device?
  - What design choices let integrated sensors work with low-cost, broadband sources such as LEDs?
---

Integrating the sensing element directly with the detector removes bulky optics and makes compact sensors possible. My early work developed integrated refractive-index sensors in which aluminum nanohole arrays are placed directly on Ge PIN photodiodes, with simulation-driven optimization of the sensor geometry. More recent work extends optical sensing to metalens-based ranging.
